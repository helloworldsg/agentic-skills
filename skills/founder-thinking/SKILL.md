# Founder Thinking

Goal: turn vague strategy into one sharp read, a practical wedge, and fast next moves.

## Inputs

Need:

- Subject: product, market, competitor, plan, funnel, operation, audience, or status quo.
- Actor: founder, team, company, product, project, or operator trying to win.
- Audience or beneficiary: customer, user, team, buyer, reader, community, etc.
- Optional: goal, stage, geography, pricing tier, time horizon, budget, constraints.

If the subject or audience is missing, ask one concise question. Otherwise proceed.

## Lens

Use the smallest set that fits the request:

- **Gap**: what others cannot or will not solve.
- **Wedge**: narrow segment to win first.
- **Leverage**: highest output for least effort.
- **Risk**: what could kill the plan.
- **Speed**: what can ship, test, or decide fastest.
- **Positioning**: why this matters now and why this actor can win.

## Method

1. Frame the arena: audience, painful job, trigger, current alternative, success metric.
2. Identify constraints: money, trust, time, attention, distribution, regulation, technical debt, brand, incentives.
3. Find asymmetric openings:
   - Incumbent or status quo trade-off.
   - Underserved segment with urgent pain.
   - Workflow step everyone tolerates but hates.
   - Distribution channel others ignore.
   - Speed, clarity, trust, or service gap.
4. Pick one wedge: segment + pain + promise + proof mechanism.
5. Return 3 moves ranked by speed first, then strategic leverage.

## Research Rule

If facts about the subject, market, pricing, releases, reviews, behavior, constraints, or customer complaints could be stale, verify with current sources before recommending. Prefer primary sources, docs, pricing pages, changelogs, support docs, customer reviews, forums, job posts, case studies, and direct project files. Cite links or file paths briefly and separate observed facts from inferred gaps.

```md
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <move> | <days/weeks/months> | <audience and constraint logic> | <test or signal> |
| 2 | <move> | <days/weeks/months> | <audience and constraint logic> | <test or signal> |
| 3 | <move> | <days/weeks/months> | <audience and constraint logic> | <test or signal> |

**Positioning line:** <plain-language claim>
**Main risk:** <risk that would invalidate the read>
**Do next:** <single fastest validation step>
```

## Quality Gates

- Moves must be concrete, legal, ethical, and executable within 12 months.
- Avoid generic advice like "improve quality" unless tied to a specific operating mechanism or test.
- Do not invent facts. Mark assumptions or research them.
- Make the recommendation decisive: one read, one wedge, three moves.
- Rank by execution speed first, then strategic leverage.
